Mouse vs Cat: Kitchen Chase

You are a mouse in somebody else’s kitchen. Outlast the clock, grab what you can on the way, and stay out of reach. Ten kitchens, one very determined cat, and a few things that should not be in a kitchen at all.

Free to play No account, no login No subscription Ads only, and they are optional Plays offline

How it plays

  • Move by touching anywhere and dragging — a joystick appears under your finger and follows it, so a short push is already most of your speed.
  • Dash with a double tap for a short burst. You are always faster than whatever is chasing you, so a dash breaks a pounce.
  • Survive the clock. That is the whole task — there is nothing you have to collect first. Levels 1 to 5 run two minutes, the rest run three.
  • Laddus are coins. Ten coins the moment you touch one, forty-five for a golden one, and the rate never changes.
  • Diamonds open the next kitchen. One drops on the floor every minute, at a different moment each run, and it does not wait around. You also earn one per star. The next kitchen costs five.
  • Pour milk to pull the nearest cat off the hunt: it drinks, then leaves the kitchen for a while. Every six laddus fills a jug, twice per level for free.
  • Dive into mouse holes to break a chase. The cats lose you completely down there and you can sit it out for up to twenty seconds — but the clock keeps running.
  • Spend diamonds in the shop on sleep spray, a speed boost or a shield, then tap them on the right edge mid-chase.

The ten kitchens

Every level is its own room with its own palette — a sunlit home kitchen, teal tiles, a marble pantry, a steel galley, a grain storeroom, a mint diner, the head chef’s kitchen, and two levels where the lights are off and all you have are your whiskers.

One cat hunts you all the way to level 8. She just gets faster and cleverer: she paths around the furniture, cuts off the hole you were sprinting for, and pounces when she gets a clear line. Level 8 is where she brings a second pair of paws.

Two things that are not cats

The snake, on level 7. It comes up out of a mouse hole and it is not hunting anybody. It drifts around with no plan and only strikes at what strays close, then forgets you the moment you break away. The cats will not go near it — which makes it cover, if you dare use it.

The kitten, on levels 9 and 10. Quicker than its mother and far worse at working out where you went. It also fits down the mouse holes, which is why they are boarded shut on those levels. Nowhere to duck.

Privacy in one line

The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect or transmit any personal data. Your progress and coins live on your own device. The only outside service is Google AdMob, which serves the optional reward ads — the details are in the Privacy Policy and the Data Use page.
