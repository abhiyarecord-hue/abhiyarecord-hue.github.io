Mouse vs Cat: Kitchen Chase

You are a mouse in somebody else’s kitchen. Fill your laddu quota, keep away from the cats and survive until the clock runs out. Ten kitchens, one very determined cat family.

Free to play No account, no login No subscription Ads only, and they are optional

How it plays

  • Move by touching anywhere and dragging — a joystick appears under your finger.
  • Dash with a double tap for a short burst. It is loud, and cats hear it.
  • Collect laddus to fill the quota shown at the top of the screen.
  • Survive the clock. Level 1 runs two minutes, each level adds one more. The clock ends the level, the quota decides whether you passed.
  • Pour milk to pull the nearest cat off the hunt: it drinks, then leaves the kitchen for a while. Every six laddus fills a jug, twice per level for free.
  • Dive into mouse holes to break a chase, and watch out for the mousetraps.

The ten kitchens

Every level is its own room with its own palette — a sunlit home kitchen, teal tiles, a marble pantry, a steel galley, a grain storeroom, a mint diner, the head chef’s kitchen, and two levels where the lights are off and all you have are your whiskers.

The cats are not on rails. They path around the furniture, come at you from separate angles, cut off the hole you were sprinting for, and pounce when they get a clear line.

Privacy in one line

The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect or transmit any personal data. Your progress and coins live on your own device. The only outside service is Google AdMob, which serves the optional reward ads — the details are in the Privacy Policy and the Data Use page.
