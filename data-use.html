Data Use

Companion to the Privacy Policy · Last updated 7 August 2026

This page spells out, item by item, what the game does with data. It matches the Data safety declaration on the Google Play listing.

No data is collected or shared by the developer

There is no sign-in, no profile, no cloud save and no server of ours. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

Data handled by the ad network

The game shows optional rewarded ads and restart interstitials through Google AdMob. Google processes technical device information to deliver and measure those ads, including the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, and whether an ad was viewed, clicked or rewarded. We never receive that information in a form that identifies you, and we cannot link it to your gameplay.

Details and controls: How Google uses data from apps using its services.

Turning ads down

  • Rewarded ads are always your choice — every level is completable without them.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID and switch off ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the consent prompt shown before ads load decides whether personalised ads are used.
