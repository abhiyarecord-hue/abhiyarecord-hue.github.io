Common questions

How do I finish a level?

Two things at once: stay alive until the clock reaches zero, and have at least the laddu quota shown at the top. Reaching the end of the clock without the quota counts as an incomplete task, and the level has to be replayed.

Why did the level not end when I hit the laddu target?

The target is only half the task. The clock decides when the level finishes — the quota decides whether you passed it. Keep collecting after you hit the target: extra laddus earn extra stars.

How does milk work?

Every six laddus fills a jug, and two jugs per level are free. Pour one and the closest cat walks over, drinks it, then leaves the kitchen for a while — the other cats keep hunting. After the two free jugs, more can be bought with coins.

How do I get coins?

Clearing levels pays coins, extra stars pay a bonus, and you can watch an optional rewarded ad from the home screen or the milk shop. There is nothing to buy with real money in this game.

An ad did not load

Rewarded and restart ads need a connection, and the ad network does not always have one ready. Nothing is lost when that happens: the game says so and lets you carry on or restart the level yourself.

I got caught. Can I carry on?

Yes. Watching a rewarded ad puts you back in the same run with your score, laddu count and clock exactly as they were. If no ad is available you can restart the level instead.

How do I reset my progress?

Home screen → New Game. It clears level progress and stars, and keeps your coins. To wipe absolutely everything, use Android’s Settings → Apps → Mouse vs Cat → Storage → Clear data, or just uninstall.

Does the game work offline?

The game itself does, completely. Only the optional ads need a connection.

Can I turn the sound or vibration off?

The SOUND toggle on the home screen switches both the sound effects and the haptic buzzes off.

Reporting a bug

Screenshots help a lot, especially for anything visual. If a cat gets stuck, or a level feels impossible, tell us the level number and what you were doing — that is usually enough for us to reproduce it.
